Zarr Leaflet Viewers

Leaflet viewers rendering multiscale zarr v3 pyramids directly in the browser (via a zarr-maps fork), streamed from CloudFront. No backend, no tiles server.

  • SWOT SSHA — Expert 2 km / Unsmoothed 250 m
    sea-surface height anomaly swaths, cycle 42; slice slider, click for value
  • MUR SST 1 km — Nov–Dec 2025
    global sea-surface temperature + anomaly; day slider, click for value (first load ~30 s)
