Zarr Leaflet Viewers

Leaflet viewers rendering multiscale zarr v3 pyramids directly in the browser (via a zarr-maps fork), streamed from CloudFront. No backend, no tiles server.

  • SWOT SSHA — Expert 2 km / Unsmoothed 250 m
    sea-surface height anomaly swaths, cycle 42; slice slider, click for value
  • MUR SST 1 km — Nov–Dec 2025
    global sea-surface temperature + anomaly; day slider, click for value (first load ~30 s)
  • MUR SST 1 km — custom WebGL layer
    same data through zarr-gl: one canvas, per-tile textures, Mercator reprojection in the shader — no antimeridian gap, no north-south drift on zoom
  • Planet Tanager hyperspectral — 426 bands, EPSG:3413
    surface reflectance from an Icechunk store, reprojected to polar stereographic; band slider steps 376–2499 nm client-side, clicking the scene plots the pixel's full spectrum
  • EMIT L2A hyperspectral — 285 bands, Pamlico Sound
    read straight out of an Icechunk v2 repo via icechunk-js, no plain-zarr copy; level 0 is EMIT's own ~60 m GLT grid, with L2A cloud/water mask overlays and click-to-spectrum
  • SWOT geostrophic currents — animated, native swath
    Windy-style particle field over one L3 pass: colour carries true speed, pale streaks carry direction. The odd one out — no zarr and no regridding, the curvilinear 2 km swath is searched directly
